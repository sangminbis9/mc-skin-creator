# Photo-led skin finish

Visual references: [NameMC eGirl](https://namemc.com/minecraft-skins/tag/egirl) ([pink waves](https://namemc.com/skin/f04d3e55cdf9b792), [muted purple locks](https://namemc.com/skin/4a7101f691376ac9)); [NameMC eBoy](https://namemc.com/minecraft-skins/tag/eboy) ([dark sweep](https://namemc.com/skin/31c0708dd7b8a33a), [brown fringe](https://namemc.com/skin/5812366f31eb9470), [minimal relief](https://namemc.com/skin/c5af48289e4de5e0)). Their base layers establish a dark-to-light volume. The outer layers need only step out at a few visible locks; many side and rear pixels deliberately remain flat. These are small design samples, not universal style rules. The renderer uses original rules, not copied textures or a live NameMC dependency.

- **Photo cues are constraints, not a pixel trace.** The underlying cut, part opening, length, side tuck, hair pigment and fixed eye map do not change with the selected finish. eGirl groups the reflection into broader ribbons and softly steps the temple relief; eBoy uses directional fringe highlights and more shadow under the sweep. Photo-led chooses a finish using the cut and texture, not the person's gender.
- **Build the volume on the outer layer.** Only hair-backed cells can rise; raise paired, hair-backed UV edges together, and follow a physical coordinate system for shading. Coils, braids, locs, tied shapes and compact cuts retain their characteristic relief. Body locks and clothes stay unchanged by the new head finish.
- **Wrap behind the fringe.** The crown and rear are sculpted in shared cuboid coordinates. Large loose locks cross adjoining faces; the rear crown corners step inward. The front mask, front shading, eye positions, length and dye remain the same for the same observation.
- **Keep the display rules.** Fixed eyes, no nose, mouth, or eyewear. Hats retain their source paint priority; full coverings use their separate finish. Earlier renderer policy IDs remain replayable.
- **No extra inference.** The web preference still survives retries and has its own versioned cache key. It is applied during the existing deterministic rendering pass after analysis.

Without sending photos, run `stylizedHairArt.test.ts` for geometry and seam coverage and `freeEngine.test.ts` for request propagation. With the offline synthetic inputs, set `SYNTHETIC_PORTRAIT_REVIEW_DIR` and `STYLIZED_HAIR_REVIEW_DIR`; `syntheticPortraitReview.test.ts` writes a front and three-quarter comparison of v5 and all three v6 finishes. This is a rendering check, not an objective likeness benchmark.

For the 128-portrait regression, run `hairPortraitBench.test.ts` with `HAIR_BENCH_DIR` pointing at the stored observations and `HAIR_BENCH_OUTPUT_DIR` pointing at a separate review directory to retain the last benchmark. It checks each cut and base mask against v5, validates the 64×64 atlas, and compares the fixed eye texels byte for byte.
